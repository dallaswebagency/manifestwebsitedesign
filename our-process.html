How we work

Our process

Every project starts with a conversation and ends with something that works hard for your business.

Here's how each one works. Whether we're building your website, growing your traffic, or setting up an AI agent, we follow a clear, proven process so you always know what's happening and why.

Service one

Our process for a website build

See website design

  1. Discovery

    We ask a lot of questions.

    We want to understand why you need a new website and what you're really trying to achieve: your goals, your customers, and what a win looks like for you.

  2. Planning & Structure

    We map out a visual sitemap that organises your information so visitors find what they need and take action.

    We also gather your assets (images, logos, and content) and flag anything we still need.

  3. Design

    This is where we disappear into the creative side and manifest a website that brings it all together.

    The look, the feel, and the practical all come together here, reflecting your brand and guiding visitors toward becoming customers.

  4. Build & Test

    Once you're happy with the design, we build it out and put it through its paces: checking it on phones, tablets, and desktops, testing every link and form, and making sure it loads fast.

  5. Launch

    When everything's polished and working, we deploy your new website so you can show it off to the world.

    We keep an eye on things straight after launch to make sure the transition is smooth.

  6. Training & Handover

    If you'd like to manage your own content, we'll show you how.

    You'll leave knowing how to make everyday updates with confidence. This is especially handy if you have a blog, projects, or gallery to keep fresh.

Service two

Our process for SEO

See local SEO

  1. Discovery & Audit

    We get to know your business, goals, and ideal customers. Then we audit your current website and rankings to see where you stand, what's holding you back, and where the biggest opportunities are hiding.

  2. Keyword & Competitor Research

    We dig into what your customers are actually searching for and how your competitors are showing up. This gives us a clear list of terms that bring the right people to your door, not just any traffic.

  3. On-Page Optimisation

    We optimise the pages on your site (titles, headings, content, images, and internal links) so search engines understand what you offer and match you to the right searches. Written for people first.

  4. Technical SEO

    Behind the scenes, we tidy up site speed, mobile performance, structure, and anything stopping search engines from crawling and indexing your site properly. A fast, healthy site is easier to rank.

  5. Local SEO & Content

    For local businesses, we make sure you show up when people nearby are searching. We optimise your Google Business Profile, build local relevance, and plan content that builds your authority over time.

  6. Reporting & Refining

    SEO isn't set and forget. We track your rankings, traffic, and enquiries, report back in plain English, and keep refining as we learn what's working. You'll always know what we're doing and what it's delivering.

Service three

Our process for AI Agent Integration

Testing happens before the agent goes live. Care carries on after it does: we keep watching and refining as real conversations come in.

See AI agent installs See what AI does in your industry

  1. Discovery

    We start with questions. We want to understand where an AI agent can genuinely help (answering enquiries, booking appointments, qualifying leads, or handling repetitive tasks) and what a good outcome looks like.

  2. Knowledge Gathering

    An AI agent is only as good as what it knows. We collect your FAQs, services, policies, pricing, and the way you like to speak to customers, so the agent sounds like you and gives accurate, useful answers.

  3. Design & Guardrails

    We map out how the agent should behave: the conversations it handles, its tone of voice, when it should hand over to a human, and what it should never do. This keeps it helpful, on-brand, and safe.

  4. Build & Train

    We build the agent and train it on your information, shape its responses so it sounds like you, and connect it to the right tools, whether that's your website, calendar, or enquiry system.

  5. Test & Refine

    Before it goes live, we put the agent through realistic conversations to find the gaps and smooth out the rough edges. We test the awkward questions, not just the easy ones.

  6. Launch & Ongoing Care

    We launch your agent and keep watching how it performs. As real conversations come in, we refine and improve it so it keeps getting better and stays accurate as your business changes.
