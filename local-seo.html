Common questions

Common questions about SEO in Australia

How long does local SEO take to show results in Australia?

You'll typically start seeing movement in rankings and organic traffic within 3 to 6 months. The compounding results - consistent inbound leads from organic search - build from month 6 to 12 and beyond.

SEO is a long game, and the businesses that treat it that way are the ones that see transformational outcomes. Anyone promising results in 30 days is either cutting corners or not being straight with you.

How much does local SEO cost in Australia?

Our SEO + Social package is $2,500 per month. That includes four blog posts, 12-16 social posts, full on-page SEO management, Google Business Profile management, monthly rank tracking with plain-English commentary, one backlink activity, and a quarterly strategy call. No lock-in contracts.

What is AI SEO and why does it matter for my business?

AI SEO is the practice of optimising your content so that AI tools - ChatGPT, Google's AI Overview, Perplexity, and others - reference and recommend your business in their answers.

When someone asks an AI assistant "who's the best commercial electrician in Sydney," the businesses it recommends aren't there by accident. They've built the right content signals over time. We build those signals into every campaign we run.

What's the difference between local SEO and general SEO?

General SEO targets broad or national keywords. Local SEO targets geographic searches - appearing in Google's Local Pack, Google Maps, and suburb or city-specific search results.

For most Australian service businesses, local SEO delivers a faster and more targeted return on investment because it puts you in front of people who are actively looking in your area.

Do you work with businesses outside the Southern Highlands?

Yes. We're based in Moss Vale in the Southern Highlands of NSW, but we work with established service businesses across all Australian states and territories - Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, and regional areas. All campaign management is done remotely with no impact on outcomes.

Can you help with Google Business Profile management?

Yes - Google Business Profile optimisation is included in every campaign. We handle weekly posts, category optimisation, image updates, review strategy, Q&A management, and ongoing profile maintenance.

Your GBP is often the first thing a local prospect sees - it needs to be working properly.
