Why it matters

What is a healthy website?

A healthy website has no broken links, still loads quickly, and keeps up with the latest requirements to rank well in Google.

It’s fast, secure, and easy for both visitors and search engines to navigate.

Having a healthy website is essential for success. It’s the foundation everything else, from SEO to conversions, is built on.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q1

How often should I get a website health check-up?

We recommend a full website and SEO health check every six months. If you're concerned about site speed, security, or a recent drop in Google rankings, we can also run more frequent mini check-ups in between.

Q2

How do website health check-ups contribute to better SEO?

A healthy, fast-loading website with no broken links and clean on-page SEO is exactly what Google favours when ranking search results. Fixing technical and SEO issues early removes the barriers that stop you from ranking higher.

Q3

What's included in a free website health check?

We check your site speed and performance, scan for broken links, review your on-page SEO, and check your Google Business Profile visibility. You'll get a full report of what we find, plus two hours of maintenance included to fix priority issues.

Q4

Do I need a website health check if my site looks fine?

Yes — most issues that hurt your Google ranking, like slow load times, broken links, or missing SEO tags, aren't visible just by looking at your site. A health check finds the problems working against you behind the scenes.
