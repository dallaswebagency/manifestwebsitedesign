The problem

Most business websites have the same problem

You spent money on a website a few years ago. It looked decent at the time. Since then, search has changed, buyer behaviour has changed, and AI tools like ChatGPT and Google's AI Overview now decide who gets recommended.

The result is a website that looks okay but does very little. Competitors with better-optimised sites show up where you don't: in Google, in AI-generated answers, and in the research phase before a prospect ever picks up the phone.

  • Lost tendersProcurement teams check your site before they call.
  • Slow enquiry periodsGood work, but the website is not pulling its weight.
  • That awkward question"I looked at your website and wasn't sure if you were still operating."

The businesses we work with usually turn over $3M to $10M. They are not short on capability. They have a website that has not kept pace with the business.

Your current site may be costing you clients you'll never know you lost.

SEO

SEO is not an add-on. It's built into every page.

Most web designers build a website and then hand it to an SEO person to "fix." We don't work that way.

Every page we build is structured for search from the first line of code - URL structure, heading hierarchy, internal linking, page speed, schema markup, meta data, and keyword targeting.

By the time your site launches, it's already optimised. Not "we'll add SEO later." Done.

  • Keyword research completed before we write a single word
  • Page titles and meta descriptions written for click-through, not just rankings
  • Schema markup included on every key page
  • Internal link structure planned as part of the site architecture
  • Core Web Vitals (speed + stability) hit from day one
AI search

We build websites that AI engines recommend

Search has changed. When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overview "who's the best commercial electrician in Sydney" - those tools don't just pull from a list of websites. They pull from sources they've determined to be credible, clear, and authoritative.

We understand how large language models assess and reference websites. We build yours to be one of the sources they trust - with clear entity signals, structured data, FAQ content, and the kind of depth that signals expertise to both Google and AI engines.

If AI tools aren't recommending your business when prospects ask questions in your category, you're already losing leads you can't see. We fix that.

Conversion

Traffic means nothing if visitors don't take action

Getting to page one of Google is only worth something if the people landing on your site actually enquire. We design every page with a clear conversion path - so a visitor who's a good fit for your business knows exactly what to do next, and is given every reason to do it.

  • Clear service pages written for decision-makers, not just Google bots
  • Trust signals (reviews, credentials, results) placed where buyers look
  • Click-to-call and enquiry forms that work on every device
  • Heatmap and analytics tracking set up at launch - so you can see what's working

Our process

How a Manifest website project works

We keep the process simple. You stay informed at every stage, and nothing moves forward without your sign-off.

  1. Discovery Call

    A 45-minute conversation where we learn how your business works, who you are trying to attract, and what you want your website to actually do. We do not build a brief without this.

  2. Strategy and Sitemap

    We map out your site structure: every page, its purpose, its target keyword and how it connects to the rest of the site. You review and approve before we move to design.

  3. Keyword Research and Copywriting

    We research the search terms your ideal clients use and write every page around them. Copy that reads naturally and ranks well.

  4. Design and Development

    We build your site to your brand: colours, fonts, imagery and layout. You review each stage and give feedback. No surprises at the end.

  5. SEO and Technical Setup

    Before we launch, we complete the full SEO setup: meta data, schema markup, Google Search Console, analytics, speed optimisation and AI search entity signals.

  6. Launch and Handover

    Your site goes live. We handle the technical side of the launch. You get a short training session so you are confident managing your own content, plus 30 days of support.

Frequently asked questions about website design

How much does a professional website design cost in Australia?

For an established service business, a professionally built website with SEO included typically starts at $8,000. At Manifest, our projects run from $8,000 to $18,000 depending on scope - number of pages, integration requirements, and whether you need copywriting. We price transparently and scope every project individually so there are no surprises.

Does SEO come included with website design?

At Manifest, yes - always. We don't build websites and hand them to an SEO person to fix later. Keyword research, on-page SEO, schema markup, meta data, and internal linking are all part of the build process. Your site is optimised before it goes live, not after.

Will my website show up in AI search tools like ChatGPT and Google's AI Overview?

That depends on how your site is built. AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overview pull from sources they've determined to be credible and authoritative. We build websites with clear entity signals, structured data, and the kind of depth that signals expertise to AI engines - so your business has the best possible chance of being recommended when someone asks an AI for a referral in your category.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most projects take 6 to 10 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on how quickly content and feedback come back. We set clear milestones at the start and keep you updated throughout. The discovery call is where we nail down a realistic timeline for your project.

Do I need to redo my website or can you improve what I have?

Honest answer: it depends. Sometimes a targeted refresh - better copy, stronger SEO, cleaner structure - is the right call. Other times the existing site is too far behind to be worth saving. We'll look at what you have and tell you which approach makes more sense before you spend anything.

What happens after my website launches?

You get 30 days of post-launch support included in every project. After that, you have a few options: manage the site yourself (we'll show you how), sign up for our website care plan, or combine both with our SEO + Social package at $2,500/month - which keeps the site growing in search and keeps content publishing consistently.
