Peace of mind

Websites are not a “set and forget” part of your business.

Ongoing website care is essential for security, and it helps with SEO

Ongoing website care is crucial for every website but most business owners don’t have the time or technical skills to do the job properly. We can take care of this for you.

As technology advances, your website needs to stay up to date. Just as you service your car or update your phone, a website needs core updates to ensure it is running at optimum capacity.

If your website and core software are not kept up to date, you run the risk of security threats.

  • Backdoors

    Hackers and viruses find the holes in the code (called backdoors) and infect your website with malware and viruses.

  • Stolen data

    They can steal your personal information and customer data.

  • Real damage

    They can do a lot of damage. Keeping your site up to date closes the gaps they rely on.
