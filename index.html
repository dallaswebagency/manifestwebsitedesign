How much does a website cost in Australia?

For an established service business, a professionally built, SEO-ready website typically starts at $5,000. At Manifest, our projects run from $5,000 to $18,000 depending on size and scope. We're transparent about pricing upfront - no surprises mid-project.

How long does it take to see results from SEO?

Honestly, SEO takes time. You'll start seeing early movement in rankings within 3-6 months. Real, compounding results - consistent leads from organic search - typically kick in at the 6-12 month mark. Anyone promising results in 30 days is either taking shortcuts or not being straight with you.

What makes a website high-converting?

A high-converting website does three things: it ranks in Google and AI search for the right terms, it loads fast and looks credible on mobile, and it makes it easy for a visitor to take the next step - call, enquire, or book. Most business websites fail at all three. We fix that.

Do I need SEO if I already have a website?

Having a website and being found on Google are two different things. Most business websites sit there unseen. SEO is what closes the gap - it makes sure the right people find you when they're actively searching for what you offer.

What is AI search optimisation?

When someone asks ChatGPT, Google's AI Overview, or Perplexity a question like 'best accountant in Sydney,' those tools pull from trusted sources across the web. AI search optimisation makes sure your business is one of those sources - so you show up in the answer, not just in the blue links below it.
