Top Industries Most Affected by SEO AI Overviews

Jillian BrandonManifest Website Design16 September 2025 6 min read

Word-of-mouth is still gold, but Google is where customers start.

Healthcare: Trust, Accuracy, and the Risks of Google AI Overview Misinformation

AI answers to medical queries can oversimplify information, cut clicks, and make clinics work harder to prove credibility.

Finance: When Generative AI Summaries Threaten Click-Throughs and Credibility

AI-generated rate summaries can overshadow financial firms' guides, so expertise and compliance signals matter more.

E-Commerce & Retail: How AI Overview Results Steal Product Visibility

AI product summaries can pull sales away from individual stores, making structured data and reviews important.

Construction: Project Leads at Risk in the Era of SEO AI Overviews

Generic AI guidance on hiring builders can push local firms down the page, and showcasing real projects helps them stand out.

HVAC: Service Calls and Seasonal Traffic Impacted by Google AI Overview

Quick DIY answers to urgent repair searches can reduce calls, while localized seasonal guides can still build trust.

Education & Learning: Generative AI vs. Expert Content in Search Rankings

Smaller schools may get buried under AI course listings, so accreditation and outcomes help them show value.

Local Services: What SEO AI Overviews Mean for Tradies, Builders, and Niche Providers

AI can either promote DIY fixes ahead of local tradespeople or highlight well-optimized businesses backed by proof.

Promotes the agency's services for Australian service businesses, including AI-aware SEO, lead-focused websites, and plain-language reporting.

Strategies to Protect Your Rankings When AI Overview Dominates the Page

Offers four practical steps: deeper content, local proof, schema and FAQs, and working with specialists.

The Bottom Line

Concludes that AI works best as a tool for making websites perform, and that businesses which adapt can grow.
