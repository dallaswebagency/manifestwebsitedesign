Meet your next set of hands

You don’t need another app. You need another set of hands.

Most business owners aren’t short on ideas. They’re short on hours. The admin piles up, the follow-ups slip, the marketing sits on the ‘one day’ list. Not because you don’t care, but because there’s only one of you.

  • The day disappears into admin

    Invoices, reminders, inbox, quotes, scheduling. Necessary work, but none of it is the work that grows your business.

  • Marketing keeps slipping

    You know you should be showing up on Google and posting on social. It just never makes it to the top of the list.

  • It all lives in your head

    Your processes, your tone, how you like things done. It’s all in your head or scattered across a dozen tools. Nothing runs without you.

  • Hiring is a big commitment

What makes it different

Viktor is not ChatGPT with a Slack skin.

Plenty of AI tools can talk. Viktor can do. Three things set it apart from every chatbot you’ve tried.

It has memory

Tell Viktor about your business once. It remembers your clients, your processes, your brand voice, and how you like things done, and uses that on every task.

  • Your clients and how you work with them
  • Your processes and the way you like things done
  • Your brand voice, on every piece of writing

It has hands

Viktor connects to Gmail, Xero, Google Drive, your CRM, your social accounts and more, then actually completes the task. It doesn’t hand you instructions. It does the job and reports back.

  • Reads data and drafts work inside your own accounts
  • Posts and sends only where you tell it to
  • Reports back when the job is done

It has a schedule

Set a routine once and Viktor runs it. Daily lead summaries, weekly reports, overdue invoice reminders, all done on time, every time, without you lifting a finger.

  • Weekly reports
  • Overdue invoice reminders

Viktor is brilliant for some businesses, and wrong for others.

Do I need any technical skills?

No. If you can send a message on your phone, you can work with Viktor. You download Slack, and we handle everything else.

What can Viktor actually connect to?

The tools you already use: email, calendar, accounting, drive, CRM, social platforms and thousands more. We map this to your business during setup.

How long does setup take?

Most clients are up and running within days, not months. We do the heavy lifting so you’re not stuck configuring anything.

What if I want to stop?

No lock-in. If it’s not working for you, we close the account out cleanly.

Will Viktor’s work sound like my business or like a robot?

That comes down to the onboarding. We spend real time loading Viktor with your tone, your services, your clients, and how you like things done. Set up properly, its work sounds like you.

How much of my time does this take each week?

Realistically, a little time reviewing and approving what Viktor drafts. It does the work; you make the calls.

Does Viktor just answer questions, or actually do things?

It does the work. Viktor researches, writes, updates your systems, creates documents and runs multi-step jobs across your tools, then reports back when it’s done. Answering questions is the least interesting thing it does.

Can Viktor handle work that repeats every week?

Yes, and this is where most clients get the biggest win. Set a routine once and Viktor runs it on schedule. Daily lead summaries, weekly reports, overdue invoice reminders, all done without you asking again.

Can Viktor send or post things without me approving them?

No, and that’s deliberate. Viktor drafts and queues, but nothing is sent or published until you approve it in Slack.

Is my business data safe?

Viktor only connects to the accounts you approve, your passwords are stored encrypted and never read by the AI, and your data is never used to train any AI model. The platform is SOC 2 certified and vetted by Slack. Data is hosted in the US. We’ll walk you through the detail on the discovery call.
